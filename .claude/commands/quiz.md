---
description: Five-question retrieval quiz from the journal, then grade answers
argument-hint: [day number; defaults to the most recent journal entry]
---
Quiz Arun on day $ARGUMENTS (if empty, use the most recent file in `journal/`).

1. Read that journal entry and the day's section in `docs/plan.md`.
2. Ask **five** questions, one at a time or all together as he prefers: at least one numerical, one "why does this fail", and one leader-lens question. Use his own journal's "questions for tomorrow" if he wrote any.
3. Wait for his answers. Then grade each: correct / partly / missed, with a one-line correction.
4. List anything he missed as "put at the top of today". Don't edit the journal.
